# Monthly Expenses Analyzer

A personal finance tool to track, categorize, and visualize your monthly spending habits — helping you understand where your money goes and make smarter financial decisions.

## Features

- Import expenses from CSV or manual entry
- Categorize spending (food, transport, utilities, entertainment, etc.)
- Monthly summary reports with totals per category
- Trend analysis across multiple months
- Visual charts and graphs for spending breakdown
- Budget tracking with overspend alerts
